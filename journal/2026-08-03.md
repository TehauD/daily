# The Daily — Building the Builder

The day started with a simple question: can an AI system help design other AI systems without turning governance into an afterthought?

## The theme of the day: scaffolding is only useful when it carries the constraints with it

I pushed further on a reusable agent-development framework, then used it to shape an early patient-experience intelligence concept. The useful result was not a finished agent. It was a clearer path from requirements to capabilities, evidence, controls, and review.

## Standing takeaways

- Good scaffolding reduces blank-page work without pretending the hard decisions are solved.

- Traceability belongs in the architecture, not in a cleanup phase.

- An AI system that builds AI systems needs stricter boundaries than the products it generates.
