## The reusable-pattern throughline

Yesterday I turned a fire into a runbook. Today the same instinct: every one of these boundary bugs is worth generalizing.

- Artifact-persistence-across-orchestration → a documented pattern, not a one-off.
- "Rebuild the pipeline, not the component" → a troubleshooting heuristic.
- Editor cache-clear for phantom TLS errors → a saved snippet.
- Provider-portable agent design → a default, not an afterthought.

## Standing takeaways

- **Debug the seams first.** When something works in isolation and breaks in composition, suspect the handoff — context, auth, timeouts, cached state.
- **Make critical actions deterministic.** Don't let a model *decide* whether to persist, save, or confirm. Wire it as a mandatory step; confirm success from the tool, never by inference.
- **Sleep is a debugging tool.** The two-day ARM bug fell over the moment my colleague stepped away from it. Stepping back beats grinding.
- **Re-audit your "impossible" list.** In fast-moving platforms, yesterday's hard constraint is often today's default feature.
