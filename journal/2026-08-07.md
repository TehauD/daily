# The Daily — From Spreadsheet to Working Surface

The interesting problems are often hiding inside ordinary requests. “Put this spreadsheet on a screen” turned into a broader workflow and product-design question.

## The theme of the day: the interface is only the visible edge of the workflow

I explored a lightweight assignment-board concept, continued metadata enrichment work, and mapped access constraints around reporting telemetry. The strongest signal was that a static display request was really asking for a reliable operational surface with refresh, access, and support expectations.

## Standing takeaways

- Translate the requested artifact into the underlying job to be done.

- A spreadsheet can be a prototype, but it should not silently become infrastructure.

- Access, refresh cadence, and ownership belong in the first design conversation.
