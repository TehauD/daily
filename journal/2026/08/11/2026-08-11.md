# The Daily — Containment Before Convenience

The hard part of external collaboration is not authentication. It is preventing convenience from quietly expanding access.

## The theme of the day: identity is not the same thing as authorization

The day centered on collaboration controls, licensing constraints, and the operational implications of modern identity patterns. Stronger sign-in does not automatically create least privilege. The architecture still has to constrain where external identities can go and how their access changes over time.

## Standing takeaways

- Treat external access as a lifecycle, not a one-time share action.

- Authentication can improve while authorization risk gets worse.

- Safe collaboration requires architecture, monitoring, and ownership together.
