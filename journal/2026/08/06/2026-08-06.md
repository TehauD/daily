# The Daily — Past the Proof of Concept

Some days end with more questions. This one produced a clean technical signal: the integration worked, the data matched the need, and the remaining work became engineering instead of discovery.

## The theme of the day: validation is the bridge from demo to delivery

A search-analytics integration moved beyond proof of concept after the output was reviewed against the original need. Around it, the day included release review, deployment-governance discussion, and an important reminder that many “AI opportunities” are better solved first with reliable automation.

## Standing takeaways

- A validated boring pipeline beats an impressive unverified demo.

- Use AI where judgment or ambiguity exists. Use deterministic automation where it does not.

- The fastest way to build trust is to make acceptance criteria visible.
