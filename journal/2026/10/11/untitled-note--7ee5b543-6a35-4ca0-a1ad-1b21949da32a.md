# The Daily Is Becoming Something Else

I think I finally understand why I keep returning to The Daily.

I keep describing it as a journal, but that doesn't feel right anymore.

A journal records.

What I'm actually chasing is accumulation.

Every conversation, prototype, requirement document, governance review, architecture diagram, dead end, and random thought disappears unless it gets connected to something else.

I've built a lot of things this year.

What I haven't always done is allow them to compound. #type/win

The Daily feels like the first thing I've worked on that might make everything else more valuable with time instead of aging into another disconnected artifact.

That feels important.

Maybe more important than some of the individual projects.
