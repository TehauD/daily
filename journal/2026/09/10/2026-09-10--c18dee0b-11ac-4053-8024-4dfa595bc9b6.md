The Daily: Local Inference Is an Operating Model

Evaluating local AI requires more than measuring model quality. It requires governance, ownership, support, economics, and security.

Today I translated those questions into a Foundry Local review package, executive decision materials, workload mappings, and continued ATA refinement. #type/learning

The theme: Match workloads to architecture tiers

Not every workload requires cloud-scale infrastructure.

Likewise, not every workload belongs on a local workstation.

Field notes from the build
