The Daily: The Ground Is Moving Beneath Every AI Roadmap

Building an AI solution used to mean selecting a platform and executing. Today the challenge is understanding how rapidly changing capabilities alter the assumptions behind that choice.

In contract-intelligence discussions, the conversation expanded from go-live readiness into a broader review of licensing models, Copilot capabilities, Foundry options, custom applications, and emerging vendor platforms. #type/learning

The theme: Re-evaluate architecture when the platform changes

The discussion highlighted how quickly AI capabilities are spreading across products.

A design that looked optimal months ago may need reevaluation when new capabilities become available.

Field notes from the build

Legal and contract-intelligence discussions increasingly focused on identifying capability gaps rather than simply validating existing assumptions.

The question shifted from 'Can we build it?' to 'Should we build it here?'

What moved

-Evaluated contract-intelligence direction against changing AI offerings.

-Reviewed interim versus future-state architecture.

-Expanded discussion around governance, readiness, and licensing.

-Identified overlap and differentiation opportunities between platforms.

The reusable pattern

Separate immediate workflow needs from long-term platform decisions.

Standing takeaways

-Platform change creates architectural debt.

-Interim solutions and future-state solutions serve different purposes.

-Governance needs to evolve alongside capability.
