# The Daily — Build the On-Ramp, Not Just the Agent

The week ended with a familiar scaling question: how do more people start building without lowering the quality bar?

## The theme of the day: enablement is part of the platform

I helped shape a concise learning path for new AI builders while also working through connector-policy needs, deployment approvals, data-access incidents, and platform-capacity warnings. The common thread was operational readiness.

## Standing takeaways

-

A five-course on-ramp can be more useful than an exhaustive catalog.

-

Builder enablement should combine fundamentals, governance, platform skills, and publishing discipline.

-

Every successful pilot eventually creates demand for support, capacity, and lifecycle management.
