# The Daily: Capacity Is Governance

Provisioning more environments without limits creates a shared-resource failure mode.

## The theme: Make consumption visible before scale

In a transcribed Power Platform baseline review, I worked through Dataverse capacity, environment allocation, data-loss-prevention policy assignment, connector coverage, and naming consistency.

The main risk was uncontrolled consumption. Individual environments could draw from shared capacity, and nonproduction use could affect the broader platform if limits and notifications were not configured.

## Field notes from the build

The working decisions were to disable capacity overages for Copilot Studio environments, enable notifications near limits, verify the intended data-loss-prevention policy, update environment-creation documentation, add required Work IQ connectors, and normalize naming.

My assigned work was to update the provisioning documentation with the new capacity and policy steps. The meeting was transcribed, so the decisions and action are directly supported. The evidence did not show that my documentation update was completed that same day. #type/win

The discussion also highlighted cost accountability. Environment requests were connected to director-level awareness, but technical controls were still necessary. Budget awareness cannot replace quotas, policy, and monitoring.

In parallel, I explored automation opportunities and continued work on governed AI and machine-learning standards. A separate town hall discussed publishing AI-generated survey summaries after leadership review. Those references supported the broader governance context, but I did not treat them as completed work by me. #type/win

I also received a package-management problem from the data-science team. Manually approving exact archives for frequently changing Python and R dependencies was not scalable. I began considering an internal artifact feed as a more maintainable control, but the documented recommendation came the following day.

## What moved

-

Participated in a transcribed capacity and policy review for Copilot Studio environments.

-

Defined controls for overages, threshold notifications, policy assignment, connectors, and naming.

-

Accepted the provisioning-documentation update for the revised environment process.

-

Surfaced shared-capacity and cost risks before additional environment growth.

-

Began framing a scalable alternative to manual data-science package approval.

## The reusable pattern

Treat platform capacity as code. Every environment request should declare owner, purpose, expected storage, connector needs, policy, expiration, and cost center. Provisioning should apply limits and alerts automatically.

## Standing takeaways

-

Shared capacity without quotas is an operational risk.

-

Governance needs technical enforcement, not only approval.

-

Package controls must account for dependency and version churn.

## Open thread

The environment process still needed updated documentation and automated verification of limits, alerts, and policies.
