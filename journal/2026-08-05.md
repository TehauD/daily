# The Daily — Small Wins, Expensive Plumbing

Today had the energy of a platform changing underneath the people building on it.

## The theme of the day: licensing, access, and architecture are part of the product

I worked across credential governance, agent requirements, connector policy, and the changing economics of AI development. A promising new development experience also introduced cost ambiguity, which forced a useful architectural question: what survives if the preferred tool or pricing model changes?

## Standing takeaways

-

Design for portability before the platform gives you a reason.

-

Documentation is a scaling mechanism when it lets others operate independently.

-

A proof of concept is not production-ready until cost and ownership are understood.
