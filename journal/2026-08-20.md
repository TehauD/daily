# The Daily — The Default Policy Is Architecture

Default settings become organizational behavior long before anyone calls them strategy.

## The theme of the day: platform baselines decide what teams can build safely

The day centered on reviewing connector policy, access requirements, and governance questions for a growing Power Platform estate. A separate reporting-security issue reinforced the risk of allowing authorization rules to live outside repeatable deployment controls.

## Standing takeaways

-

Defaults should express the approved architecture, not historical accident.

-

Connector governance needs a clear route for standard, premium, and exceptional cases.

-

Security configuration should be versioned, testable, and recoverable.
