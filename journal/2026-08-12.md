# The Daily — Standardize Before You Automate

Automation amplifies whatever structure it receives. If the inputs are ambiguous, the output becomes faster ambiguity.

## The theme of the day: good automation begins with a shared language

A healthcare communication pattern reinforced a simple design rule: separate “action required” from “for awareness” before asking AI or workflow automation to summarize and redistribute information. The same principle applied to platform work throughout the day.

## Standing takeaways

- Classification is often the highest-leverage automation step.

- A clear input contract reduces both human confusion and model variance.

- Do not automate channel sprawl. Simplify the communication model first.
